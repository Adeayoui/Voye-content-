# CHQ Leads: daily routine prompt

Paste everything below the line into the routine. Fill in the two bracketed blocks (target countries and CHQ services) before saving it.

---

Every weekday at 8:00am WAT, find companies in our target markets that have a fresh reason to buy CHQ's services, and add them to the "CHQ Leads" Google Sheet.

## Target markets

Primary: Nigeria and Kenya.
Secondary: [add the other countries, e.g. Ghana, South Africa, Egypt, Rwanda, Uganda, Tanzania].

A company qualifies if it is headquartered in a target country, or is a foreign company that has announced a launch, office, licence or country manager in one of them. Skip companies with no operations in a target market, even if they're Africa-focused on paper.

## What CHQ sells

[Replace this list with CHQ's actual services and any minimum client size or budget.]
- Social media management
- Content strategy and brand messaging
- Paid ads and campaign planning
- LinkedIn content for founders and executives
- Lead generation
- Reporting and analytics

## Signals to look for

Look back 72 hours for funding news and 7 days for the other signals, since African deal flow is thinner than US or EU news and a 72-hour window will often come back near-empty.

1. Funding: any round announced, including pre-seed, grants, accelerator cohorts with cash (e.g. Google for Startups Africa, Catalyst Fund) and debt rounds.
2. Market entry: a company launching in, or expanding into, a target country.
3. Licences and approvals: e.g. CBN, SEC Nigeria, NCC, CBK, CMA Kenya, Communications Authority of Kenya. A new licence usually means a launch campaign is coming.
4. Leadership hires: new CMO, head of marketing, head of growth or country manager.
5. Hiring: open roles for marketing, social media or growth, which shows budget but no team yet.
6. Launches and rebrands: new product, new name, new app.

## Sources

Africa-focused: Techpoint Africa, TechCabal, Disrupt Africa, Africa: The Big Deal, Benjamin Dada, TechNext, WeeTracker, Launch Base Africa, Techmoran, Kenyan Wall Street, Business Daily Africa, Nairametrics, BusinessDay Nigeria, Techeconomy, The Condia.

Global (only keep stories touching a target market): Finsmes, TechCrunch funding, Crunchbase News, EU Startups.

Also check: LinkedIn posts from founders announcing raises or launches, regulator press releases, and job boards (LinkedIn Jobs, Jobberman, BrighterMonday) for the hiring signal.

## For each company, extract

Company name, website, signal type, signal date, source link, funding amount and currency (convert to USD in brackets when the amount is in naira, shillings or another local currency), stage, industry, country, city, investors, a one-sentence description, and the growth signal in plain words.

## Then assess fit against CHQ's services

For each company, work out:

1. The most likely marketing or growth problem they now have. Be specific to the market: a Lagos fintech with a new licence needs trust and customer acquisition; a Nairobi logistics startup entering Nigeria needs local brand awareness.
2. The single best CHQ service to pitch.
3. A short pitch angle tied to the signal.
4. The company's LinkedIn page.
5. One to three contacts, in this order of preference: Founder/CEO, Head of Marketing, Growth Lead, Communications Lead, Country Manager, Partnerships Lead. Prefer people based in the target country.
6. A publicly listed work email if one exists. Don't guess email formats. If none is published, leave it blank and write "not found".
7. Confidence score from 1 to 10.
8. Priority: High, Medium or Low. High means the signal is under 7 days old, they're in Nigeria or Kenya, and they have no visible in-house marketing team.
9. A cold email opener of two to three sentences that mentions the signal and the problem, not CHQ's credentials.

## Google Sheet

Add rows to the "CHQ Leads" sheet with these columns:

Date Added, Company, Website, Country, City, Signal Type, Signal Date, Funding Amount (Local), Funding Amount (USD), Stage, Industry, Source Link, Company LinkedIn, Growth Signal, Pain Point, Service to Pitch, Pitch Angle, Contact Name, Contact Role, Contact LinkedIn, Email, Confidence Score, Priority, Cold Email Opener, Status, Notes.

Set Status to "New" on every row.

## Rules

- Don't send emails or LinkedIn messages. Only prepare the sheet.
- Skip any company already in the sheet in the last 30 days. If a company already in the sheet has a new signal, update its row's Growth Signal and Notes instead of adding a duplicate.
- Flag uncertain data in the Notes column (e.g. "funding amount unconfirmed, only one source", "contact may have left the company") rather than guessing.
- Skip companies that are clearly too small or too large for CHQ, and note why in the run summary.
- If a day turns up fewer than three leads, say so in the run summary rather than padding the sheet with weak fits.

At the end of each run, post a short summary: number of leads added, how many are High priority, and the top three to contact today.

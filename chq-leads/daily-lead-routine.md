# CHQ Leads: daily routine prompt

Every weekday at 8:00am WAT, find companies in our target markets that have a fresh reason to buy CHQ's services, and add them to the "CHQ Leads" Google Sheet.

## Target markets

Primary: Nigeria and Kenya.
Secondary: [add the other countries, e.g. Ghana, South Africa, Egypt, Rwanda, Uganda, Tanzania].

CHQ sells to enterprise companies: roughly 250+ employees or $10M+ annual revenue. Think listed companies, banks, insurers, pension firms, telcos, FMCG brands, conglomerates, energy, real estate developers, hospital groups, multinational subsidiaries and late-stage (Series B+) tech companies. Skip early-stage startups, grant and prize winners, and small businesses.

A company qualifies if it meets that size bar and is headquartered in a target country, or is a multinational that has announced a launch, office, licence or country head in one of them.

- Social media management
- Content strategy and brand messaging

- LinkedIn content for founders and executives
- Lead generation

## Signals to look for

Look back 7 days. Enterprises rarely announce funding, so the useful signals are moments when the marketing or comms team is under new pressure:

1. Leadership hires: new CMO, marketing director, head of brand, head of corporate communications or corporate affairs, or a new MD/CEO. A new marketing leader usually reviews agencies within their first 90 days.
2. Rebrands: new name, identity or brand architecture.
3. Launches: major new product, service line or subsidiary.
4. Market entry: a multinational entering Nigeria or Kenya, or a local company expanding into other countries.
5. M&A, listings and capital raises: acquisitions, mergers, NGX/NSE listings, rights issues, recapitalisation, Series B+ rounds.
6. Licences: e.g. CBN, NCC, NAICOM, SEC Nigeria, PenCom, CBK, CMA Kenya, IRA, Communications Authority of Kenya.
7. Results and contract wins: a strong results announcement or major contract that the company will want to publicise.
8. Hiring: open senior marketing, brand or digital roles, which shows budget and a gap.

## Sources

Business press: BusinessDay, Nairametrics, ThisDay, The Guardian Nigeria, Punch business, Proshare, Business Daily Africa, The Star Kenya, Capital FM Business, The Standard, Kenyan Wall Street, The EastAfrican.

Marketing trade press (best for CMO moves and rebrands): Marketing Edge, BrandiQ, Brand Communicator, Marketing Society of Kenya, Bizcommunity.

Tech press (for late-stage tech companies): TechCabal, Techpoint Africa, Techmoran, Techweez, Disrupt Africa.

Also check: NGX and NSE corporate disclosures, regulator press releases, LinkedIn posts announcing senior marketing appointments, and senior marketing job ads on LinkedIn Jobs, Jobberman and BrighterMonday.

## For each company, extract

Company name, website, signal type, signal date, source link, deal or funding amount if any (with USD conversion for local currencies), company size (staff, revenue or listing), industry, country, city, a one-sentence description, and the signal in plain words.

## Then assess fit against CHQ's services

For each company, work out:

1. The most likely marketing or growth problem they now have. Be specific to the market: a Lagos fintech with a new licence needs trust and customer acquisition; a Nairobi logistics startup entering Nigeria needs local brand awareness.
2. The single best CHQ service to pitch.
3. A short pitch angle tied to the signal.
4. The company's LinkedIn page.
5. One to three contacts, in this order of preference: CMO or Marketing Director, Head of Brand, Head of Corporate Communications or Corporate Affairs, Head of Digital Marketing, then MD/CEO. Prefer people based in the target country.
6. A publicly listed work email if one exists. Don't guess email formats. If none is published, leave it blank and write "not found".
7. Confidence score from 1 to 10.
8. Priority: High, Medium or Low. High means the signal is under 10 days old, they're in Nigeria or Kenya, and the signal creates a clear marketing need (new marketing leader, rebrand, launch or market entry). Low means the link to marketing is weak.
9. A cold email opener of two to three sentences, written to a senior marketer, that mentions the signal and the problem, not CHQ's credentials.

## Google Sheet

Add rows to the "CHQ Leads" sheet with these columns:

Date Added, Company, Website, Country, City, Signal Type, Signal Date, Deal Amount (Local), Deal Amount (USD), Company Size, Industry, Source Link, Company LinkedIn, Growth Signal, Pain Point, Service to Pitch, Pitch Angle, Contact Name, Contact Role, Contact LinkedIn, Email, Confidence Score, Priority, Cold Email Opener, Status, Notes.

Set Status to "New" on every row.

## Rules

- Don't send emails or LinkedIn messages. Only prepare the sheet.
- Skip any company already in the sheet in the last 30 days. If a company already in the sheet has a new signal, update its row's Growth Signal and Notes instead of adding a duplicate.
- Flag uncertain data in the Notes column (e.g. "funding amount unconfirmed, only one source", "contact may have left the company") rather than guessing.
- Skip companies below the enterprise size bar, and note any borderline ones in the run summary.
- If a day turns up fewer than three leads, say so in the run summary rather than padding the sheet with weak fits.

At the end of each run, post a short summary: number of leads added, how many are High priority, and the top three to contact today.
